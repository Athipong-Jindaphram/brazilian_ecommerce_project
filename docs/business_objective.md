# Business Objective

## Background

This project analyzes a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace dataset to understand customer behavior, product performance, sales trends, and business operations.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making.

---

## Business Questions

1. What are the overall sales and revenue trends?

2. Which products generate the highest revenue?

3. Which product categories perform the best?

4. Which sellers contribute the most to total sales?

5. What factors influence customer satisfaction?

6. How do payment methods affect purchasing behavior?

7. Which customers are the most valuable?

8. What is the order fulfillment performance?

9. Which regions generate the highest sales?

10. What business opportunities can be identified from the data?

---

## Expected Deliverables

- Clean and validated dataset
- PostgreSQL database
- SQL analytical queries
- Statistical analysis
- Interactive Power BI dashboards
- Business recommendations
